1. Explain how your design supports active learning, safety, accessibility, and classroom 
management.  
This classroom design supports active learning by providing both collaborative round 
tables and individual desk areas. It promotes safety through clear walkways and 
unobstructed access to exits. The spacious layout ensures accessibility for all learners, 
while the organized seating arrangement allows the teacher to easily monitor students 
and manage classroom activities. 
2. Explain the reasons behind your design choices and how your layout can help create 
a classroom that is organized, safe, accessible, and conducive to learning  
The layout was designed to balance group collaboration and independent learning. 
Round tables encourage discussion and teamwork, while desk clusters support focused 
tasks. Wide aisles improve movement, safety, and accessibility. Overall, the classroom 
is organized, safe, flexible, and conducive to learning, helping students stay engaged 
and productive.
