Teaching is a profession that requires a unique set of personal qualities and 
professional traits. As educators, teachers influence not only the academic growth of 
students but also the development of their character and life values. Personal teacher 
attributes refer to the individual qualities, attitudes, and characteristics that define a 
teacher’s personality. These include empathy, patience, kindness, integrity, resilience, 
and the genuine love and passion for helping others learn.  
In contrast, a professional teachers attributes are the skills, knowledge, and behaviors 
expected of educators within the teaching profession. These include subject or topic 
expertise, instructional competence, effective communication, classroom 
management skills, adherence to educational policies, commitment to continuous 
professional development, and ethical decision making. 
I believe that ethical behavior is the main building blocks of effective teaching. A 
teacher serves as a role model whose actions could mean communicate more 
powerfully than words. That’s why, educators must demonstrate honesty, fairness, 
respect, and accountability in all professional interactions. 
One of my core beliefs is that every student deserves equal opportunities to learn 
regardless of background, ability, culture, or socioeconomic status. Teachers avoid 
favoritism and ensure that classroom decisions are based on fairness and the best 
interests of learners.  
To establish an effective and positive classroom environment, I intend to utilize my 
personal strengths of empathy, patience, adaptability, and optimism. I believe that a 
supportive classroom begins with strong teacher-student relationships. Therefore, I 
will make a deliberate effort to know my students individually, learn about their 
interests and needs, and create opportunities for open communication.
